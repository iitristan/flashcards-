### Item 2

**Question**: Overboiling of egg will result to ___.

**Choices**:
- [ ] **A**. Formation of acrolein
- [x] **B**. Coagulation *(Correct Answer)*
- [ ] **C**. Gelatinization
- [ ] **D**. Rancidity

**Verified Answer**: Coagulation

**Rationale**: Excessive heat causes overcoagulation of egg white and yolk proteins, causing syneresis and the formation of a dark grayish-green ferrous sulfide ring at the yolk-white interface.

> **Exam Notes & Margin Analysis**: PRC NDLE Oct 2021 Set B, Item 2. Margin notes: Acrolein = overheating fat; Gelatinization = cereal/starch; Rancidity = spoilage in oil.
