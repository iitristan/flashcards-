## Examination Overview & Domain Breakdown

The October 2021 Nutritional Biochemistry and Clinical Dietetics (NBCD) Board Examination contains 200 multiple-choice questions assessing practical and theoretical competency across:
1. **Medical Nutrition Therapy (MNT):** Renal diets (hemodialysis vs. pre-dialysis, nephrotic syndrome), Cardiovascular diets (CHF, hypertension sodium guidelines, hypercholesterolemia, NCEP Step diets), Gastrointestinal disorders (peptic ulcer, gastroparesis, diverticular disease, celiac disease, dumping syndrome, ileostomy, IBS), Endocrine & Diabetes (glycemic goals, exercise protocols, hypoglycemia, exchange calculation for alcohol), Oncology & Pulmonary (cancer cachexia, cytotoxic therapy symptoms, COPD & respiratory failure RQ management).
2. **Nutritional Biochemistry & Metabolism:** Intermediary hepatic metabolism, urea cycle and ammonia detoxification, bioenergetics & ATP, gluconeogenesis, essential fatty acids (cis vs. trans, PUFAs, MUFAs), antioxidant enzymes (catalase, Vit E), amino acid fuels (glutamine, BCAAs).
3. **Vitamins & Minerals:** Fat-soluble vs water-soluble properties, Vitamin A visual cycle, Vitamin D secosteroid hormone status, Vitamin K intestinal bacterial synthesis, Niacin from tryptophan, energy-scaled vitamins (B1, B2, B3), trace minerals (Chromium GTF, Zinc, Iron absorption enhancement).
4. **Clinical Dietetics, Nutrition Support & Assessment:** Nutrition Focused Physical Exam (NFPE), handgrip dynamometry, visceral protein classification (albumin depletion cutoffs), bioelectrical impedance (BIA), hydrostatic weighing, enteral tube feeding indications/contraindications, formula safety and preparation, refeeding syndrome triad.
5. **Food Exchange Lists (PEL) & Dietary Planning:** Philippine Exchange List calculations (Item 100 protein calculation, Item 188 fat exchange calculation, meat/fat exchange rules), food labeling ('reduced' claims), allergy elimination, behavioral counseling models (SMART objectives, ABC model, Social Learning Theory, Prochaska Stages of Change).

### Question 1
**Prompt:**  
Diet-related components that may be protective against CHD include all of the following, EXCEPT:

**Options:**  
- A. antioxidant nutrients, particularly vit. E
- B. fiber
- C. B vitamins, particularly folate
- D. vitamin K

**Correct Answer:**  
`D. vitamin K`

**Clinical Rationale & Key Takeaways:**  
Cardioprotective dietary components include antioxidant nutrients like Vitamin E (which inhibits LDL oxidation), soluble fiber (which binds bile acids and lowers serum total and LDL cholesterol), and B-vitamins such as folate, B6, and B12 (which lower circulating homocysteine, an independent vascular risk factor). Vitamin K functions primarily in hepatic synthesis of blood clotting factors (II, VII, IX, X) and bone mineralization (osteocalcin carboxylation), and is not classified as a CHD-protective nutrient.

**Exam Notes & Calculations:**  
Exam marked: D. Vitamin K. Margin: CHD protective nutrients include Vit E (antioxidant), fiber (bile binding), folate (homocysteine reduction).
